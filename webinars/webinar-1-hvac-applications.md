## Description
This webinar is CPD-certified through Grundfos Holding A/S.

We'll explore how hydronic chilled water systems are at the core of efficient building cooling — and how the right pumping design choices directly impact energy performance, system reliability, and long-term sustainability across Saudi Arabia.

Our speakers **Niren Rohanlal, Senior Sales Development Manager at Grundfos**, and **Tariq Dewali, Sales Development Engineer at Grundfos**, will share practical insights into how intelligent pumping solutions support high-performance HVAC systems, enabling energy savings, operational efficiency, and lower lifecycle costs.

## What you will learn
### Hydronic system configurations and chiller plant design
Understand the fundamentals of constant flow, primary/secondary, and variable primary flow systems — and how selecting the right configuration improves chiller performance and reduces energy waste.

### Diagnosing and preventing Low Delta-T Syndrome
Learn how Low Delta-T Syndrome develops in HVAC plants, why it forces chillers to work harder than necessary, and how correct pumping design and valve selection can eliminate it entirely.

### Pump protection and long-term system reliability
Explore the critical role of minimum bypass valves, differential pressure sensors, air separators, and expansion tanks in safeguarding your pumping assets and maintaining peak system performance over the asset lifecycle.
